Career first. Then university.

Three steps, at your pace. Your parents can be part of every one.

  1. A free call

    Tell us where you are and what’s on your mind. We answer your questions and suggest what would help. No cost, no commitment.

  2. Find your direction

    In your private discovery space you collect clues about yourself, one card at a time. You talk to people doing the work and try it in small ways. Then your counsellor turns it all into your Future Map with you.

  3. Choose and apply

    Now you pick the courses, universities and countries that fit that direction. We help with your statement and every deadline and, if you’re moving abroad, the visa, housing and your first weeks.

Why students and parents trust Yondo

  • Universities don’t pay us.

    Many free study-abroad services earn a commission from the universities they recommend. We take none, so our advice is only about what fits you.

  • Your parents are part of it.

    We talk with them separately, so their hopes and worries shape the plan too. If you’re under 18, we ask their permission first.
