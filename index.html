What happens after you message us

No test to prepare for, and nothing to sign before you’ve talked to us. You can stop after any step.

  1. You message us

    On WhatsApp or by email. Tell us who’s exploring and what’s on your mind. A few lines is plenty.

  2. A free first conversation

    With a Yondo counsellor. We ask about you, not just your marks, and explain how Yondo works. No pressure, and no perfect answers needed.

  3. Your private discovery space

    If you go ahead, you’re invited to a private online space: short questions and small challenges, one card at a time. Under 18? A parent’s permission comes first.

  4. Your family’s view

    We talk with your parents separately, so their hopes, worries and limits are heard too.

  5. Your consultation and Future Map

    We go through it all with you: a few directions worth testing, what each really involves, and what to try next.

  6. Then, if you want us with you

    Shortlisting universities, applications, your visa, getting ready to fly and your first weeks abroad.

Your own private discovery space

After your first conversation, your counsellor invites you to a private online space. Short questions and small challenges, one card at a time, on your phone or laptop. “I don’t know yet” counts as an answer, and your counsellor reads everything you send.

The people behind Yondo

We built Yondo because we went through these decisions ourselves, across India, Singapore, Dubai and beyond.

Harrsh Agrawal

Co-founder, Yondo

An education entrepreneur and operator who has worked across schools, coaching, student programmes and education ventures. He studied in India, Singapore and Dubai. Working with institutions and students, he has seen how much young people are capable of, and how little real exposure most of them get. Yondo is his attempt to close that gap.

Tushar Tayal

Co-founder, Yondo

An entrepreneur, global mobility professional and youth leader. He founded Travelidea, graduated as valedictorian from SP Jain School of Global Management, and secured All India Rank 1 in the CBSE AISSCE examination in 2019. His work keeps coming back to one problem: people have the ambition to move across borders, but not the access, guidance or pathways to do it well.
