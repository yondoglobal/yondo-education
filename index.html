Why families choose Yondo

  • Universities don’t pay us.

    Many free study-abroad services earn a commission from the universities they recommend. We take none, so our advice is only about what fits you.

  • Discovery comes before applications.

    We help you work out what you want first, so the university list is built around you.

  • Parents are part of it.

    We talk with parents separately, so their hopes, worries and limits shape the plan too.

  • We stay until you’ve settled.

    Visa, flights, accommodation and your first weeks abroad, not just the offer letter.

Questions families ask us first

Students ask

What should I even study?
We start with what you enjoy and how you learn, not just your marks, then test a few directions for real.
Which country is right for me?
It depends on the course, costs, visa rules and where you’d enjoy living. We compare them side by side.
What if I choose wrong?
That’s why you try things first: conversations and small projects come before years and fees.

Parents ask

Is it worth the investment?
We set tuition, living costs and scholarships against where graduates actually go, so you can judge it properly.
Is it safe?
Accommodation compared safely, an arrival plan, and local safety information and contacts for the first weeks.
What does it cost?
The first conversation is free. Fees for the programme and for application support are explained in it, before you decide anything.
