okay, but what actually happens?

The steps

Discovery comes first. Applications and moving come after, when you know what you’re aiming for.

  1. You message us

    On WhatsApp or by email. Tell us who’s exploring and what’s on your mind. A few lines is plenty.

  2. A free first conversation

    With a Yondo counsellor. We ask about you, not just your marks, and explain how Yondo works. No pressure, and no perfect answers needed.

  3. Your private discovery space

    If you go ahead, you’re invited to a private online space: short questions and small challenges, one card at a time. Under 18? A parent’s permission comes first.

  4. Your family’s view

    We talk with your parents separately, so their hopes, worries and limits are heard too.

  5. Your consultation and Future Map

    We go through it all with you: a few directions worth testing, what each really involves, and what to try next.

  6. Then, if you want us with you

    Shortlisting universities, applications, your visa, getting ready to fly and your first weeks abroad.

What you’ll actually do along the way

  • Talk to people doing the work

    Real conversations with people living the careers you’re considering, so you hear what the work is like day to day.

  • Try things before you commit

    Small projects, simulations, shadowing or challenges, before you spend years and savings on a choice.

  • See more of the world

    Cities, cultures, campuses and opportunities beyond where you are now.

  • Leave with a Future Map

    A few directions worth testing, and the next experiments to try.

What it costs

The first conversation is free.No forms, no pressure.

If you’d like to continue, we’ll explain the fees for the full programme, and for application and moving support, before you decide anything.
