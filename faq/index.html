wait, one more thing…

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 91110 00194 or email hello@yondo.global, and we’ll set it up.

What does the full programme cost?

We explain the fees for the full programme, and for application and moving support, in your first conversation, before you decide anything.

Is this a personality test that tells me my career?

No. We don’t hand you one career. We help you find a few directions worth exploring, and ways to test them before you commit.

Do I need to know what I want before I start?

No. You don’t need to have life figured out. That’s what the first conversation is for.

Who is Yondo Education for?

Students in high school, students who’ve finished school and students already at university, and their families. Schools and universities can work with us too.

Do you help with applications and visas?

Yes. Once you know what you’re aiming for, we help with shortlisting and comparing universities, applications, your visa, accommodation, flights and your first weeks abroad. See how.

How does the private discovery space work?

After your first conversation, your counsellor sends you an invitation. There’s no public sign-up. You answer one card at a time, and every answer is saved to your private record, so you can pause and carry on from any device.

Will I get a score or result straight away?

No. Nothing is scored or labelled on screen. Your counsellor reads what you send and talks it through with you in your consultation.

What if I don’t know an answer?

Choose “I don’t know yet”, “I haven’t tried this”, “Discuss with my counsellor first” or “Skip for now”. These are real answers and stay visible as open questions.

Can I change an answer after sending a chapter?

Yes. Add a correction and send it again. Your earlier answer is kept and the update is linked to it, so nothing is lost.

Who can see my answers?

Your counsellor, and any other counsellor you’ve specifically agreed to. A family summary is agreed with you first. Your answers are never shown to other students.

I’m under 18. What do my parents need to do?

Your counsellor confirms a parent or guardian’s permission before your answers are collected. No identity document is needed.

Can I stop taking part?

Yes, at any time, from “Privacy choices” in the discovery space or by telling your counsellor. Your answers then stop being used for new reviews.

I’ve forgotten my password.

Ask your counsellor to reset it for you.

Where are you based, and can I start from anywhere?

Yondo Education is registered in Sharjah Media City (Shams), UAE. The first conversation starts on WhatsApp, so you can message us from wherever you are.

Can you guarantee an admission, a scholarship or a visa?

No one honestly can. Universities, scholarship bodies and governments make those decisions. We help you choose well, prepare strong applications and get every document right.

Who runs Yondo Education?
