Where we help

Four stages, in order. Most students need help with some of them, not all.

Choose

Rankings tell you about a university. Fit tells you about your life there.

  • Universities compared on six kinds of fit: academic, financial, career, lifestyle, location and personal
  • Tuition, living and hidden costs side by side
  • Scholarships and deadlines you can realistically meet
  • Where graduates go, and how quickly
  • A summary your family can review together

Apply

Less chasing paperwork, more time telling your story.

  • One timeline for submissions, scholarships and interviews
  • Passport, transcripts, tests and statements, each with a clear status
  • Honest feedback on your statement
  • Reminders for your referees
  • Conditions, deposits and next steps as soon as you hear back

Get ready to go

The scariest part of moving is not knowing what comes next, so we lay it out in order.

  • Country-specific visa steps, documents and dates
  • Halls and private accommodation, compared safely
  • Flights and an arrival plan
  • Insurance, banking and staying connected
  • A pre-departure session before you fly

Settle in

The practical things, done in order, so you can start actually living there.

  • Your first two weeks, step by step
  • SIM card, bank account and a transport card
  • Registering with a doctor and knowing where to go
  • University registration, ID card and orientation
  • Local safety information and emergency contacts
