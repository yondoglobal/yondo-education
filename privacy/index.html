who can see my answers?

Your answers are yours.

How the discovery space looks after what you share, in plain language.

Who we are

Yondo Education is a brand of Bindal Univentures LLC, Sharjah Media City (Shams), UAE. Licence no. 2433960.01, issued by Sharjah Media City (Shams).

What we collect

  • The answers you choose to give in the discovery space, including “I don’t know yet” and other open answers.
  • Your privacy choices (age band, taking part, family summary preference) and any permission your counsellor records.
  • The email address used to sign in, and when you save or send a chapter.

We ask you not to share ID numbers, passwords, bank details or unrelated medical information, and the space never asks for them.

Why

To help you and your counsellor explore possible directions and plan next steps. Nothing is scored or labelled automatically.

Who can see it

  • Your counsellor, and any other counsellor you’ve specifically agreed to.
  • A family summary is agreed with you first. Information your family shares is recorded separately and isn’t shown to you without agreement.
  • Your answers are never shown to other students, sold, or used for advertising. These pages use no tracking or advertising scripts.

Students under 18

Your counsellor confirms a parent or guardian’s permission before any answers are collected.

How long it’s kept

Your counsellor explains the retention period before you begin. You can ask your counsellor to show, correct or delete your information.

Stopping

You can withdraw at any time from “Privacy choices” in the discovery space or by telling your counsellor. Your answers then stop being used for new reviews. Copies already shared with your counsellor are handled as they explain.

Where it’s stored

The discovery space runs on Supabase, in its Mumbai (ap-south-1) region. Answers are saved only after the server confirms them, and only signed-in, invited people can reach them.

WhatsApp and email

If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we use what you send only to reply and to arrange your conversation.
