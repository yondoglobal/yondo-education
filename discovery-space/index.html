An example card

A free Saturday. Which of these would you actually enjoy?

Noted. There’s no score and no right answer. Your counsellor looks at your answers together, not one at a time.

That’s a real answer. It stays on your record as an open question to explore together, not a blank.

What’s inside

Your counsellor opens chapters as you go. Some open straight away; others open after you’ve talked.

  1. Your launchpad

    Your school, subjects, timing and what you’d like help with first.

  2. Collect your clues

    What you enjoy, what you’re curious about and what drains you.

  3. Your learning story

    How you learn best, and what your results do and don’t say about you.

  4. Find your kind of place

    Cities, climates, campus life and how far from home feels right.

  5. Small challenges

    Short tasks. Try them on your own and tell us how they felt.

  6. Try two kinds of work

    Pick two of eight mini-experiences and see what fits.

  7. Your Future Map

    Opened with your counsellor after your consultation.

Before you begin

  • You answer three short privacy questions: your age band, whether you’d like to take part, and how a family summary should be handled.
  • If you’re under 18, your counsellor confirms a parent or guardian’s permission first. No identity document is needed.
  • It works on a phone, tablet or computer, and you can pause and carry on from any device.

Please don’t share

  • Passwords or sign-in codes
  • Aadhaar, PAN, passport or other ID numbers
  • Bank or card details
  • Medical information that isn’t relevant

The discovery space is for planning, not urgent help. If something can’t wait, contact your counsellor directly.
