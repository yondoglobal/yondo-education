The people behind Yondo

Harrsh Agrawal

Co-founder, Yondo

An education entrepreneur and operator who has worked across schools, coaching, student programmes and education ventures. He studied in India, Singapore and Dubai. Working with institutions and students, he has seen how much young people are capable of, and how little real exposure most of them get. Yondo is his attempt to close that gap.

Tushar Tayal

Co-founder, Yondo

An entrepreneur, global mobility professional and youth leader. He founded Travelidea, graduated as valedictorian from SP Jain School of Global Management, and secured All India Rank 1 in the CBSE AISSCE examination in 2019. His work keeps coming back to one problem: people have the ambition to move across borders, but not the access, guidance or pathways to do it well.

How we work

  • We begin with you

    Not with a university’s commission.

  • We explore before we recommend

    Possibilities come before applications.

  • Parents stay part of it

    Without taking the decision away from the student.

  • Reality matters

    Conversations with people doing the work count as much as brochures.

  • Global doesn’t mean one destination

    The right place matters more than the fashionable one.

  • Growth doesn’t stop at graduation

    University is one chapter in a much longer life.

Why “Yondo”

“Yonder” means somewhere beyond where you are today: another possibility, another place, another version of yourself. Yondo helps young people discover themselves, explore what’s out there, and make decisions they can stand behind.
